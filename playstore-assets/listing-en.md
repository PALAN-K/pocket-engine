## Full Description (4000 chars max)

CCTV cameras, baby monitors, smart home hubs, personal servers...
Are you safely managing devices that run 24/7?

PocketMonitor is a device health guardian that monitors your always-on device in real time and sends you daily safety reports.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

▶ Device Health Score
Our algorithm analyzes temperature, CPU, memory, storage, and battery to generate a 0–100 health score. See your device status at a glance.

▶ Real-Time Monitoring
• CPU usage — catch overload instantly
• Memory usage — check available space
• Storage — detect low space early
• Temperature — track overheating risk in real time
• Battery — level, voltage, charging status & type

▶ 24-Hour History Charts
View temperature, CPU, and memory trends on 24-hour graphs. Warning lines at 45°C and 50°C help you spot danger zones easily.

▶ Daily Safety Report (Push Notification)
Every morning at 9 AM, receive a device status summary.
"Average temp 38°C, CPU 12%, Battery 85%"
One daily check is all you need to keep your device safe.

▶ Temperature Alerts
• Above 45°C: Warning notification
• Above 50°C: Critical urgent alert
Temperature management is the #1 concern when running old smartphones 24/7. PocketMonitor alerts you immediately when overheating is detected.

▶ Weekly Summary
Every Sunday, get a weekly device status report.

▶ Extensions
Connect with PocketEngine extension tools for even more capabilities. Check Settings > Extensions for details.
